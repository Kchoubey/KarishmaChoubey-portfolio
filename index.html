Product Philosophy

Problem first. Solution second.

The products I enjoy most sit in complex environments. My job is to make the complexity understandable, make the right tradeoffs and help teams move toward a customer outcome.

01

Diagnose before designing

I combine research, data and frontline signals to understand why a journey is failing before deciding what to build.

02

Let the problem choose the technology

AI, automation, traditional UX and human support are tools. The customer problem determines which one belongs in the experience.

03

Make complexity invisible

Payments, enterprise systems and regulations can be complicated behind the scenes without making customers feel that complexity.

04

Align before accelerating

Strong product outcomes come from bringing Business, Design, Engineering, Compliance and Operations around the same customer problem.

A Little More About Me

I like hard problems with human consequences.

My career started close to technology and delivery, but my curiosity increasingly moved toward the product questions behind the work: why are we building this, whose problem are we solving and what would make the experience genuinely better?

That curiosity led me into product management. I enjoy starting with a customer problem, combining qualitative insight with data, framing the opportunity and bringing different disciplines together around a clear product direction.

I am particularly passionate about AI powered customer experiences, self service, fintech and payments, and products that make complicated workflows feel simple. I care about the details, but I care even more about whether the product creates meaningful value for the person using it.

What I want to solve next

Complex problems where technology can make the customer experience dramatically simpler.

AI powered customer experiences, fintech and payments, self service and enterprise products are the spaces where I am most excited to keep learning and building.
