How I think

Problem first.
Solution second.

01

Diagnose before designing

I use customer research, behavioral data and frontline feedback to understand why an experience is failing before deciding what to build.

02

Let the problem choose the technology

Sometimes the answer is AI. Sometimes automation. Sometimes a smarter UX flow. The solution follows the problem.

03

Make complexity feel simple

I translate regulation, enterprise constraints and technical complexity into intuitive customer-facing experiences.

04

Align the whole system

Business, Design, Legal & Compliance, Technology and Customer teams need a shared outcome — not just a shared roadmap.
